## Requirement

"Limit use of portable storage devices on external systems."

## Objectives

- [a] the use of portable storage devices containing CUI on external systems is identified and documented;
- [b] limits on the use of portable storage devices containing CUI on external systems are defined; and
- [c] the use of portable storage devices containing CUI on external systems is limited as defined.

## Sufficient

- **Rely on a written policy and user training rather than a technical control**, naming which users are authorized to move CUI-holding portable media and what they may and may not do with it outside the organization's own systems; assessors have accepted this administrative approach in practice. [a][b][c]
- **State the prohibition in plain terms users recognize**, such as never inserting an organization-owned CUI storage device into a personal computer or a public kiosk, paired with training confirming users understand it. [b][c]
- **Run a check-out and check-in process for physical media leaving the controlled environment**, limited to authorized users, with a log documenting each cycle. [a][c]

## Insufficient

- **Treating device-level encryption alone as satisfying this requirement**: encryption protects the data on the device, it does nothing to stop an authorized user from plugging that same encrypted device into an external system, which is the behavior this requirement limits. [c]
- **Deploying endpoint agent software that blocks unauthorized removable media only on organization-managed systems**: that software has no reach onto the external systems this requirement is actually concerned with, since no agent is installed there to enforce anything. [c]

## Over-engineering

No documented position beyond the Assessment Guide; see confidence.

## Edge cases

- What counts as "external" here is read broadly in practice: it covers systems outside the organization entirely, but it can also cover a system inside the organization that sits outside the CUI-handling environment, such as a general business system kept separate from the CUI enclave.
- Whether this requirement limits only organization-owned media, or reaches any portable storage device regardless of ownership, is an open reading; the requirement's own text does not specify ownership, leaving room to argue it covers personal media brought in by an authorized user as well.
- A technical, system-binding control for portable media, one that only unlocks on an approved system using an encrypted enterprise-managed device or a certificate match, exists and was discussed as an option, but administrative policy and training alone have been accepted without it, so a technical control is a strengthening measure rather than a requirement.

## Evidence examples

- Policy identifying which users may take CUI-holding portable storage outside the controlled environment and under what conditions. [a][b]
- Training record showing users were instructed on portable-media restrictions before being authorized to handle CUI media. [b]
- Check-out and check-in log for portable media leaving and returning to the controlled environment. [a][c]
- Inventory of approved portable storage devices and, where used, any external systems approved for their use. [a]

## Related requirements

- 3.8.1 and 3.8.2 protect CUI media generally and limit access to it, of which this requirement is the external-system-specific case.
- 3.1.20 governs the external systems themselves that this requirement's limits on portable media are meant to keep CUI away from.
- 3.13.11 would apply if a technical, encryption-based control were chosen to enforce this requirement instead of policy and training.
