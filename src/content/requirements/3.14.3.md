## Requirement

"Monitor system security alerts and advisories and take action in response."

## Objectives

[a] response actions to system security alerts and advisories are identified;
[b] system security alerts and advisories are monitored; and
[c] actions in response to system security alerts and advisories are taken.

## Sufficient

- **Subscribing to vendor and industry advisory feeds**, such as an email distribution list or an RSS feed, and reviewing them on a cadence tied to how often the source publishes. Assessment Guide discussion points to reputable external sources, including a national computer emergency readiness function, software vendors, and industry information-sharing groups. [a][b]
- **A documented list of trusted alert sources scoped to the organization's own industry and technology**, rather than a generic subscription list. [a][b]
- **Turning an applicable external alert into an internal notice routed to personnel with a need to know**, followed by a documented response such as a configuration change or an architecture adjustment. [c]
- **Assessment Guide example: reviewing advisory emails and subscription alerts on a weekly basis**, listing which ones apply, and tracking the resulting remediation work through change management. [a][b][c]

## Evidence examples

- **A documented list of subscribed alert sources** relevant to the systems and technology in use. [a]
- **Retained alert or advisory emails with review notes** showing which were judged applicable. [b]
- **A tracked plan or ticket showing the response action taken** for an applicable alert, routed through change management where a configuration or architecture change was needed. [a][c]

## Related requirements

- **3.14.1** turns a flaw that a security alert points to into a defined identify-report-correct cycle.
- **3.11.2** supplies vulnerability scan results that help prioritize which alerts warrant action first.
- **3.6.1** picks up incident response procedures when a monitored alert turns into a confirmed incident.
