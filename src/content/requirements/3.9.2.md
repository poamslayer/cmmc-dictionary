## Requirement

"Ensure that organizational systems containing CUI are protected during and after personnel actions such as terminations and transfers."

## Objectives

[a] a policy and/or process for terminating system access and any credentials coincident with personnel actions is established;
[b] system access and credentials are terminated consistent with personnel actions such as termination or transfer; and
[c] the system is protected during and after personnel transfer actions.

## Sufficient

- **Retrieving company equipment, badges, and keys, and holding an exit interview that reminds the departing individual of their CUI nondisclosure obligations**, at the point of termination. Assessment Guide example. [a][b]
- **HR notifying IT of a termination or transfer effective date, with account disabling, physical access list removal, and credential revocation carried out on or ahead of that date.** Assessment Guide discussion. [a][b]
- **For a transfer, closing the prior role's access and provisioning the new role's access on the official transfer date**, rather than leaving both sets of access active. Assessment Guide example. [c]

## Insufficient

- **No policy or defined process describing how access and credentials get terminated when a personnel action happens.** Objective [a] asks for that process to exist before it can be carried out. [a]
- **Leaving system access active past the personnel action's effective date**, particularly where the Assessment Guide discussion calls out timely execution as essential for an individual terminated for cause. [b]

## Over-engineering

- **No documented position beyond the Assessment Guide; see confidence.**

## Edge cases

- **Exit interviews are not always possible**, for example where a termination follows job abandonment, illness, or the unavailability of a supervisor; the Assessment Guide discussion treats the exit interview as a normal practice rather than a strict pass/fail gate on its own, so its absence in these situations does not by itself defeat the objective as long as access and credentials are still terminated.

## Evidence examples

- **A termination or transfer checklist showing equipment return, badge and key return, and exit interview completion.** [a][b]
- **An IT ticket or account-disable record timestamped to the personnel action's effective date.** [b]
- **An access list or credential inventory showing an individual removed at termination, or moved, at a role change.** [b][c]
- **A transfer record showing the prior role's access closed and the new role's access provisioned on the transfer date.** [c]

## Related requirements

- **3.9.1** screens an individual before CUI access is granted; this requirement governs the other end of that same access lifecycle, when the access ends or changes.
- **3.5.1**, which this requirement's Assessment Guide discussion cross-references, depends on this requirement to keep the identified-user list current as people leave or move.
