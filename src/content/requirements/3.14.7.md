## Requirement

"Identify unauthorized use of organizational systems."

## Objectives

[a] authorized use of the system is defined; and
[b] unauthorized use of the system is identified.

## Sufficient

- **A written acceptable-use policy stating what counts as authorized use.** Satisfies the definition objective on its own. [a]
- **Detection scoped to how technical a given violation actually is.** A technical control is expected where a violation is inherently technical to detect, while a policy paired with training is accepted evidence for prohibitions that are not. [b]
- **For a technical restriction, such as which applications may run on a workstation, a screenshot of the installed application list showing only approved software.** Demonstrates detection by showing the absence of anything unauthorized. [b]
- **For a device-level restriction, such as limiting remote access to authorized hardware, device authentication controls paired with connection log monitoring.** [b]

## Insufficient

No documented position beyond the Assessment Guide; see confidence.

## Over-engineering

- **Building a distinct technical detection mechanism for every line item in an acceptable-use policy.** Not required; a policy and training pairing is accepted evidence for prohibitions that are not inherently technical to detect. [b]

## Edge cases

- **A non-technical prohibition, such as not photographing a work screen, realistically depends on an incident report rather than a technical control**, since there is no way to technically prove an unobserved action never happened. Policy and training remain the accepted evidence for that category.

## Evidence examples

- **A written acceptable-use policy** defining permitted system use. [a]
- **Training records** showing the acceptable-use policy was communicated to personnel. [a]
- **A screenshot of installed applications** on a restricted-use workstation showing no unauthorized software. [b]
- **Device authentication configuration combined with connection log monitoring** for a remote-access control point. [b]

## Related requirements

- **3.1.1** and access control establish the authorization baseline this requirement's use-monitoring checks against.
- **3.3.1** supplies the audit records that unauthorized-use detection often relies on.
- **3.14.6** overlaps where unauthorized use surfaces as unusual inbound or outbound traffic.
