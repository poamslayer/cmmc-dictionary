## Requirement

"Control and manage physical access devices."

## Objectives

[a] physical access devices are identified;
[b] physical access devices are controlled; and
[c] physical access devices are managed.

## Sufficient

- **Badges and keys treated as the core physical access devices in scope**, with key distribution to perimeter doors kept to a minimum to limit re-keying when someone leaves without returning theirs. [a][b]
- **Layered access control across building entry, elevators, and stairwells**, each independently access-controlled rather than relying on one perimeter check alone. [b][c]
- **Keycard-restricted access limited to floors or areas where CUI is processed.** [b]
- **Personnel screening for everyone who can reach a shared area, combined with access control on that area**, satisfying the objective without a fully enclosed room. [b]

## Insufficient

- **A cubicle area doing CUI work that is open to unscreened foot traffic.** Signage or floor markings alone do not resolve that exposure. [b]

## Over-engineering

- **Building full-height walls with locking doors around every cubicle doing CUI work.** An alternative safeguard, such as a policy requiring a locked screen when someone unauthorized approaches, can substitute for a physical card-access build-out, even though a card system offers stronger assurance. [b][c]

## Edge cases

- **Whether a cubicle area needs full physical enclosure when unscreened staff still have floor access is not settled**; a compensating safeguard may be enough, though this question runs closer to the authorized-individuals question in 3.10.1 than to this requirement's device-management focus, so the outcome depends on the assessor.
- **The Assessment Guide does not sharply separate "controlled" [b] from "managed" [c].** In practice, "controlled" reads as limiting who holds or can use a device, and "managed" as the lifecycle work of issuing, tracking, and revoking it.

## Evidence examples

- **A keycard or badge access system covering building entry, elevators, and stairwells.** [b]
- **An inventory of access devices** listing keys, badges, and fobs and who holds each. [a]
- **A key issuance and return log.** [a][c]
- **Personnel screening records for individuals with access to a CUI-adjacent shared area.** [b]

## Related requirements

- **3.10.1** sets the authorized-individuals population; this requirement manages the physical devices, keys and badges, that carry out that authorization.
- **3.10.2** relies on the same locks and card readers this requirement inventories and controls to protect the facility and infrastructure.
- **3.9.1** supplies the screening record that lets a shared-area occupant be documented as authorized rather than restricted.
