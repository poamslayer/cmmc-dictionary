## Requirement

"Limit access to CUI on system media to authorized users."

## Objectives

[a] access to CUI on system media is limited to authorized users.

## Sufficient

- **Actual secure storage plus direct physical control of the media, not a policy statement alone.** 32 CFR Part 2002 calls for a controlled environment built from physical barriers and procedural controls; practitioner consensus reads that as requiring the media itself to sit in secure storage, or to remain in someone's direct physical possession, whenever it is not stored. [a]
- **Restricting media access to people who are authorized to see the CUI data on it, separately from whoever is authorized to physically handle the device.** Practitioner consensus distinguishes handling authority from data-access authority: staff who may move or store the media are not automatically authorized to read what is on it. [a]

## Insufficient

- **A written policy that only instructs unauthorized users not to use CUI-bearing media, with no accompanying storage or access mechanism.** Practitioner consensus treats this as inadequate on its own; the requirement needs the physical and procedural controls behind the policy, not the policy statement by itself. [a]

## Over-engineering

No documented position beyond the Assessment Guide; see confidence.

## Edge cases

- **IT staff who are authorized to physically handle or transport media under 3.8.1 but are not authorized to access the CUI stored on it under 3.8.2.** Practitioner consensus treats this as a meaningful distinction an SSP should reflect separately, rather than assuming physical-handling authorization implies data-access authorization.

## Evidence examples

- **Access authorization list** naming who may read CUI on a given media type. [a]
- **Secure storage location** (locked drawer, cabinet, or media library) paired with a checkout log. [a]
- **Role distinction documentation** separating physical-handling authorization from data-access authorization for IT and support staff. [a]

## Related requirements

- **3.8.1** covers the physical control and secure storage of the media itself; this requirement covers who may access the CUI once that media is secured.
- **3.8.5** extends the same access-control logic to media once it is in transport outside a controlled area.
- **3.1.1** and **3.1.2** supply the underlying authorized-user determination this requirement applies to media specifically.
