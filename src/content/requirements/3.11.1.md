## Requirement

"Periodically assess the risk to organizational operations (including mission, functions, image, or reputation), organizational assets, and individuals, resulting from the operation of organizational systems and the associated processing, storage, or transmission of CUI."

## Objectives

[a] the frequency to assess risk to organizational operations, organizational assets, and individuals is defined; and
[b] risk to organizational operations, organizational assets, and individuals resulting from the operation of an organizational system that processes, stores, or transmits CUI is assessed with the defined frequency.

## Sufficient

- **A written review that spans every asset touching the CUI environment**, not a narrow slice of it. Practitioner consensus treats the review's scope as the full set of things that can affect CUI security. [b]
- **Reusing an existing company-wide risk review when the CUI environment shares infrastructure with the rest of the company**, such as a shared datacenter or shared power systems, rather than standing up a parallel duplicate exercise for the same physical assets. [b]
- **A review aimed at what changed because CUI is now present**: new exposure to unauthorized access, disclosure, or spills that did not exist before the organization began handling CUI. [b]
- **An explicit, written assessment frequency stated in policy**, so the cadence is a defined fact rather than an undefined word. Assessment Guide discussion ties the requirement to a defined interval before objective [b] can be measured against it. [a]
- **A methodology consistent with published risk assessment guidance**, applied at the organization, mission, or system level. [b]

## Insufficient

- **A review that only covers general organizational risk without addressing the exposure CUI specifically introduces.** A broad enterprise risk exercise that never asks what changed once CUI entered the environment does not satisfy the objective the requirement is scoped to. [b]
- **No stated frequency for the review.** Objective [a] requires a defined interval before objective [b], the assessment performed against that interval, can be evaluated at all. [a]

## Over-engineering

- **Treating physical and environmental hazards, such as natural disasters or infrastructure failure, as the primary focus of this review.** Assessment Guide discussion lists external events among the general categories of organizational risk, but practitioner consensus narrows this specific requirement's intent to risk that grows because the system processes, stores, or transmits CUI, since objective [b] is scoped to that CUI-driven exposure rather than to general continuity planning. [b]
- **Standing up a second, CUI-only risk assessment process when one enterprise-wide process already reaches the same infrastructure.** Duplicating the exercise for shared assets spends effort without adding coverage. [b]

## Edge cases

- **How much weight general business continuity risk should carry inside this review.** Assessment Guide discussion frames organizational risk broadly, covering process failure, technology failure, and external events, while practitioner consensus treats the requirement's real center of gravity as CUI-driven exposure. Most organizations resolve this by keeping a general risk process for continuity concerns and layering a CUI-focused lens on top of it for this requirement, rather than treating the two as separate obligations.
- **How this requirement relates to vulnerability scanning under 3.11.2.** Assessment Guide discussion states plainly that a risk assessment differs from a vulnerability assessment: a vulnerability scan supplies one input to a risk assessment, alongside likelihood analysis and threat source analysis, but the two are not substitutes for each other.

## Evidence examples

- **A documented risk assessment methodology**, whether adapted from a published risk assessment framework or built internally, showing how threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact are weighed. [b]
- **Written policy stating the defined review frequency.** [a]
- **Dated risk assessment results or review records** produced at that defined interval. [a][b]
- **Findings language that names CUI-specific exposure**, such as unauthorized access, disclosure, or spill scenarios, rather than only generic risk categories. [b]
- **A record showing the review covers the full set of assets touching the CUI environment**, including shared infrastructure carried over from an enterprise-wide review. [b]

## Related requirements

- **3.11.2** supplies vulnerability scan results as one input into this requirement's broader risk picture, but the two assessments answer different questions and neither substitutes for the other.
- **3.11.3** draws on this requirement's risk conclusions to decide how urgently an identified vulnerability needs remediation.
- **3.12.2** turns findings surfaced by this requirement's periodic review into a tracked plan of action.
