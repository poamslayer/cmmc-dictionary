## Requirement

"Limit unsuccessful logon attempts."

## Objectives

[a] the means of limiting unsuccessful logon attempts is defined; and
[b] the defined means of limiting unsuccessful logon attempts is implemented.

## Edge cases

- **Cloud services that do not offer a native account-lockout capability.** A platform lacking any built-in way to lock an account after repeated failed logons creates a real gap for this requirement. Discussion of this scenario raised two unresolved compensating directions rather than a settled answer: restricting which endpoints may even reach the service, so unlimited guessing cannot be attempted from arbitrary locations, and evaluating the full architecture together, since a companion environment with its own working lockout mechanism may cover part of the same user population. Neither direction was confirmed as sufficient on its own in the source material, and the full architecture, including how the service is reached and what else is in scope, is what would drive an assessor's read of the gap.

## Related requirements

- **3.5.2** supplies the identification and authentication mechanism that this requirement's lockout responds to; the two are typically documented together.
- **3.1.10** and **3.1.11** are the other access-control mechanisms that respond to an idle or otherwise anomalous session, alongside this requirement's response to repeated failed logons.
- **3.1.1** limits who is authorized to log on in the first place; this requirement addresses what happens when someone fails to authenticate as that authorized user repeatedly.
